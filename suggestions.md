# Cooperation Page Placement Options

## 1. "Get in touch" button area (left sidebar)
Add a subtle secondary link like "How I work" or "Working with me" underneath the CTA button. People already interested enough to look at contact will naturally want to know the terms.

## 2. Connect section
Next to "Schedule a call", add a text link like "See how we can collaborate" or "Learn about my process". Sits right where someone is making a decision.

## 3. Experience/Journey section
After the timeline, a line like "Curious how I work with clients?" feels organic — they've just read your story, now here's how to actually engage.

## 4. Project footers
At the end of each case study page, after they've seen the quality of work: "Like what you see? Here's how we can work together."

## Recommended approach
Combine option 1 + 2: a small link near "Get in touch" for the curious, and another in the Connect section for people who've scrolled the whole way. Both feel low-pressure and contextually right.
